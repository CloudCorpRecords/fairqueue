      How it works

      • The queue becomes a rotation: one song per person per round.
      • Members who have heard least so far lead the rotation — your own song order never changes.
      • Nobody gets a second song in a round while someone else is still waiting for a first.
      • Hosts can toggle it per Jam; listeners see exactly why a song moved.
      Every reorder explains itself here — no silent shuffling.
